Hi, I'm Jason Liu. One campus activity I organized was a hands-on AI builder session for students who were curious about technology but did not know how to start. The goal was simple: make the first step small enough that everyone could finish it. I prepared a short walkthrough, helped participants choose a problem, then guided them from idea to a basic prototype plan. Instead of giving a long lecture, I structured the session around three actions: define a user problem, design the smallest demo, and explain it clearly in one minute. People could ask questions while working, so the room felt like a build night. The main thing I learned is that student communities grow when the organizer removes friction. For Solana University, I would use the same format: beginner-friendly workshops, weekly building time, and a demo day where students can show useful payments, agent, or consumer app prototypes.

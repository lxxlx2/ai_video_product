Hi, I'm Jason Liu. One thing I built and shipped is a local AI platform that turns my Mac into a private developer assistant. The problem was simple: cloud tools are powerful, but access, cost, and network conditions can change. I wanted a system that could keep helping me draft, test, summarize, and run structured workflows from my own machine. I installed and benchmarked a local Qwen model, exposed a local API, connected it to my project workflow, and used GitHub to track progress and changes. The useful part is the operating process around the model: reliable startup, tests, logging, fallbacks, and a repeatable way to turn ideas into working tools. This matters for Solana because builders need fast feedback. If selected, I would bring the same practical style to campus: less theory first, more demos, more shipping, and more students leaving each session with something that actually runs.
